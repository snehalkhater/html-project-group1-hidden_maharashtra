Discover the magical villlages of Maharashtra, surrounded by lush green forests, misty mountains, and peaceful valleys. Explore nature's hidden beauty and find your next adventure.

Bhorigiri Village

Bhorgiri is a beautiful village located in Khed taluka of Pune district, Maharashtra. Surrounded by green hills and the scenic landscapes of the Sahyadri mountain range, the village is an interesting destination for people who love nature, history, and traditional village life. One of its major attractions is Bhorgiri Fort, a small historic hill fort situated on a hill near the village. The fort area contains ancient rock-cut caves, water tanks, and remains of old structures that reflect the region's historical importance. Bhorgiri is also known for its connection to the forest route leading towards Bhimashankar.

Bhorgiri is a small yet distinct village nestled in the Khed tehsil of Pune district, within the Indian state of Maharashtra. Bhorgiri is known for its charm, contributes to the cultural and demographic richness of the Pune district.

Tapola Village

Tapola is a picturesque village located near Mahabaleshwar in Satara district, Maharashtra. It is popularly known as the "Mini Kashmir of Maharashtra" because of its beautiful lake, green hills, and peaceful natural surroundings. One of its main attractions is Shivsagar Lake, an extensive reservoir formed by the backwaters of Koyna Dam. Tapola is a popular destination for nature lovers, photographers, and people seeking a peaceful escape from busy city life. Visitors can enjoy the scenic views, explore the surrounding countryside, and experience boating and other water activities where available.

Tapola is awesome for adventure lovers, with lots of water sports on the Koyna River. There’s kayaking, swimming, and even trekking to Utteshwar Temple. These are big hits with schools and groups.The best time to come here is during the monsoon, from July to September.

Samrada Village

Samrad is a beautiful village located in Akole taluka of Ahilyanagar district, Maharashtra. Surrounded by the magnificent Sahyadri mountain ranges, the village is known for its natural surroundings and peaceful rural atmosphere. It is a good starting point for visitors interested in exploring the nearby Sandhan Valley, a dramatic rocky gorge surrounded by towering cliffs. The village offers an opportunity to appreciate rural Maharashtra, its traditional lifestyle, and its close connection with nature. The surrounding mountains, rocky landscapes, and green valleys make the area especially attractive during the monsoon and cooler months.

Samrad is a village located in Akole tehsil of Ahilyanagar district, Maharashtra, India. It is approximately 65 km from Akola and around 190 km from Ahmadnagar. Samrad is also a designated Village Panchayat, headed by an elected Sarpanch.

Bhira Village

Bhira is a scenic village in Raigad district of Maharashtra, surrounded by green hills, forests, and beautiful natural landscapes. The village is particularly known for Bhira Dam and its connection with Devkund Waterfall, a picturesque waterfall located in the surrounding forest region. These attractions make Bhira an interesting destination for people who enjoy nature, photography, and exploring lesser-known places. The village also offers an opportunity to learn about rural life in the Konkan region. Its natural surroundings make Bhira an excellent addition to a travel guide featuring Maharashtra's hidden destinations.

The undiscovered beauty of Bhira has a rural feel to it. The majority of the budget is earmarked for power projects. The rest of Bhira comprises the Kundalika River, the Bhira Dam, the Devkund waterfall, and lush green forests.

Purushwadi

Purushwadi is a small village located in Akole taluka of Ahilyanagar district, Maharashtra. It is famous for its natural beauty, traditional village atmosphere, and seasonal appearance of fireflies. During the period before the monsoon, fireflies may illuminate the surrounding trees and fields at night, creating a fascinating natural spectacle. This seasonal phenomenon has made Purushwadi a distinctive destination for nature lovers and people interested in rural tourism. The village is also known for community-based tourism, in which visitors can experience local hospitality, traditional food, and village life. The surrounding countryside offers an opportunity to appreciate the natural environment and learn about the relationship between local communities and their surroundings.

This tribal village is inhabited by the ‘Hindu Mahadeo Koli’ tribe. Renowned for as rice cultivators and as skilled animal herdsmen; these villagers are warm, simple & full of wonder.
