Explore the Hidden Waterfalls of Maharashtra

Discover the magical waterfalls of Maharashtra, surrounded by lush green forests, misty mountains, and peaceful valleys. Explore nature's hidden beauty and find your next adventure.

Thoseghar Waterfall

Thoseghar Waterfall is a beautiful waterfall located near Satara, Maharashtra. It is surrounded by green hills, forests, and peaceful natural scenery. Thoseghar waterfall is the pride of the Konkan region near Satara. Located in the village of Thoseghar (where it gets the name from), this is one of the locations on your checklist if you visit Maharashtra in the monsoons.

Best known for its scenic views during the monsoon, Thoseghar is a wonderful destination for nature lovers and photography enthusiasts.The best time to visit Thoseghar is from August to October.

Bhambavli Vajrai Waterfall

Bhambavli Vajrai Waterfall is the tallest waterfall of India. The height of this waterfall is 1840 ft (560m) and it falls from a straight cliff having three steps (stages). The water for waterfall comes from the river Urmodi. It is origin of river Urmodi. The waterfall is situated in district of Satara in western Maharashtra. It is about 5 km away from well-known Kas Flower Valley and 2 km away from Bhambavli Flower Valley.Vajrai Waterfall is located near Bhambavli village in Satara district. It is famous for its impressive height and stunning natural surroundings.

The waterfall looks especially beautiful in the rainy season, with lush greenery and misty hills creating a magical atmosphere.

Devkund Waterfall

Devkund Waterfall is a scenic waterfall near Bhira village in Raigad district, Maharashtra. It is admired for its beautiful water and forest surroundings.Devkund is the origin of the Kundalika River, fed by the Tamhini ghat, and during the monsoon the falls thunder down in full force — keep a waterproof cover handy for your phone and camera. Once you reach the pool you can dip your tired feet in the cool turquoise water, enjoy the natural fish spa, and soak in the calm.

The journey to Devkund is popular among adventure lovers. Visit only when access is officially open and follow local safety instructions.

Kalu Waterfall

Kalu Waterfall is a spectacular waterfall in the Malshej Ghat region of Maharashtra. It is surrounded by dramatic cliffs, green valleys, and beautiful hills.The majestic Kalu Waterfall, located amidst the breathtaking landscapes of Maharashtra, is a hidden gem that beckons adventurers and nature enthusiasts alike. This awe-inspiring natural wonder offers a thrilling trekking experience, with its cascading waters, lush greenery, and captivating surroundings.

During the monsoon, the waterfall creates a breathtaking natural view. It is a lovely place for nature lovers who enjoy exploring Maharashtra.
