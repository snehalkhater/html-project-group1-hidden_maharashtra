History of Arnala Fort

              Arnala Fort is a historic sea fort located on an island near Arnala village in the Palghar district of Maharashtra, India. It is also known as Jaldurg and Janjire Arnala because it is surrounded by water. The fort was originally built in 1516 by Sultan Mahmud Begda of Gujarat. Due to its strategic location near the mouth of the Vaitarna River, it was important for controlling coastal routes and maritime activities along the Konkan coast. In 1530, the Portuguese took control of the island and later developed the fortifications. The fort remained under Portuguese control for nearly two centuries and served as an important naval stronghold. In 1737, during the Maratha campaign against the Portuguese, Maratha forces led by commanders associated with Chimaji Appa captured Arnala Fort. Under Peshwa Bajirao I, the fort was repaired or rebuilt, strengthening its role in Maratha coastal defence. The British later gained control of the fort during the Anglo-Maratha conflicts in the early nineteenth century. Despite changes in power, Arnala Fort remains an important example of Maharashtra's coastal military architecture. Today, visitors can explore its historic gateways, massive defensive walls, temples, and ancient water tank. Arnala Fort stands as a reminder of the maritime history and cultural heritage of the Konkan region.

Features of Arnala Fort

1. Island Fort: Located on an island near Arnala village in Palghar district.
2. Historic Gateway: Features an impressive stone entrance with decorative carvings.
3. Massive Fort Walls: Strong defensive walls and bastions protected the island.
4. Octagonal Water Tank: An important historic water-storage structure.
5. Ancient Temples: The fort contains temples dedicated to deities including Trimbakeshwar and Bhavani Mata.
6. Maratha Heritage: The fort played an important role during the Maratha campaign against the Portuguese.
7. Portuguese Architecture: Its fortifications reflect different phases of coastal military construction.
8. Scenic Surroundings: The island setting offers views of the surrounding coastal landscape.
