About Hidden Maharashtra

Discover the Unexplored Beauty of Maharashtra!

Welcome to Hidden Maharashtra, your secret travel guide to discovering the hidden gems of Maharashtra. From peaceful villages and beautiful waterfalls to historic forts, ancient temples, green hills and delicious traditional food, explore the places that make Maharashtra truly special.

Our website helps travellers find new destinations, explore local traditions, discover scenic routes and plan memorable journeys with family and friends.

                                                                        Our Mission

                                                 Our mission is to inspire travellers to explore lesser-known destinations and experience the rich culture, history and natural beauty of Maharashtra.

                                                                         Find travel inspiration, discover new destinations, and make your journey unforgettable.
