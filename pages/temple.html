Kedareshwar Cave Temple (Paachnai)

Kedareshwar Cave Temple is located near Harishchandragad in Maharashtra. It is situated inside a natural cave and houses a massive Shiva lingam surrounded by cold water.Towards the right of Harishchandreshwar temple, there is the huge cave of Kedareshwar (see picture), in which there is a big Shiva Linga, which is completely surrounded by water. Its height from the base is 5 feet (1.5 meters), and the water is waist-deep. It is quite difficult to reach the Shiva Linga because the water is ice-cold.

According to local legend, four pillars once supported the cave, but three have broken. The legend says that the world will end when the final pillar falls. The cave is known for its mysterious atmosphere and natural beauty.

Gondeshwar Temple

The Gondeshwar temple was built during the rule of the Seuna (Yadava) dynasty, and is variously dated to either the 11th or the 12th century. Sinnar was a stronghold of the dynasty during their pre-imperial period, and modern historians identify it with Seunapura, a town established by the Yadava king Seuna-chandra.

The Gondeshwar temple is built in the Bhumija style, in form of a panchayatana complex, which features a main shrine surrounded by four subsidiary shrines. The temple is situated on a rectangular platform that measures 125 x 95 feet. The plan of the temple is very similar to that of the Ambarnath Shiva temple, but the sculptures on its exterior walls are of inferior quality than those of the Ambarnath temple. The temple complex was originally surrounded by a wall, which is now mostly destroyed.

Bhuleshwar Temple (Malshiras)

Bhuleshwar Temple is an ancient hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, located near Pune, Maharashtra. It is known for its beautiful stone carvings and distinctive architectural style.The Bhuleshwar Temple is a Hindu temple of Shiva, situated around 45 kilometres from Pune and 10 km from Pune-Solapur highway from Yawat in Maharashtra, India. The temple is situated on a hill and was built in the 8th century. There are classical carvings on the walls. It has been declared as a protected monument

The temple is believed to date back to the 13th century. Its architecture combines distinctive decorative elements, and it is associated with the rare depiction of Ganeshwari, a female form of Lord Ganesha.

Shri Kopeshwar Temple (Khidrapur)

Shri Kopeshwar Temple is located in Khidrapur, Kolhapur district, Maharashtra, on the banks of the Krishna River. It is admired for its remarkable stone architecture and historical importance.It was built in the 12th century by Shilahara king Gandaraditya between 1109 and 1178 CE. It is to the east of Kolhapur, ancient & artistic on the bank of the Krishna river. Even though Silaharas were Jain kings, they built and renovated various Hindu temples, thus depicting their respect and love for all religions. Kopeshwar means angry Shiva .

The temple is famous for its open-roof Swargamandapa, also known as the heavenly hall. Its walls and pillars feature detailed carvings of elephants, deities, and other decorative figures.

Nageshwar Cave Temple (Met Indawli)

Nageshwar Cave Temple is described as a hidden Shiva temple in a cliffside cave near Met Indawli in the Satara region of Maharashtra.Nageshwar Cave Temple is a fascinating religious destination nestled in the Sahyadri mountain ranges of Maharashtra. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, this sacred shrine is situated inside a natural rocky cave in a remote and beautiful forest region associated with the Vasota Fort area. The temple is known for its peaceful atmosphere, rocky surroundings, and connection with the natural beauty of the Western Ghats. One of the distinctive features of the cave temple is the Shiva Lingam located inside the cave. Regional descriptions also mention water droplets falling from the cave's roof onto the Shiva Lingam, adding to the spiritual significance of the place for devotees. The surrounding landscape includes forested hills, deep valleys, and impressive mountain views.

Surrounded by forest and scenic landscapes, the destination is associated with a trek through natural trails. Visitors should check local access conditions and follow safety instructions before planning a visit.
