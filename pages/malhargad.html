History of Malhargad Fort

              Malhargad Fort, also known as Sonori Fort, is a historic hill fort located near Sonori village in the Pune district of Maharashtra. It was constructed around 1757-1758 during the Peshwa period and is considered one of the last forts built by the Marathas. The fort was strategically important for keeping watch over the surrounding region and protecting nearby routes. Its stone walls, bastions, and fortifications reflect the military architecture of the Maratha era. Today, Malhargad Fort is a popular trekking destination where visitors can explore its historical ruins and enjoy beautiful views of the surrounding hills and countryside.

Features of Malhargad Fort

1. Historical Significance: A historic hill fort associated with the Peshwa period and the Maratha Empire.
2. Scenic Views: Offers beautiful panoramic views of the surrounding hills and countryside.
3. Stone Fortifications: Features historic stone walls, bastions, and remnants of the entrance gate.
4. Popular Trekking Destination: A favourite spot for trekking, hiking, and adventure enthusiasts.
5. Peaceful Environment: Ideal for nature lovers and history enthusiasts seeking a calm atmosphere.
